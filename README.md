# Peening Coverage Simulators

Home page of interactive educational simulators of peening coverage, by **Dr Régis Kubler**, Arts et Métiers Institute of Technology, developed with the assistance of Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), 2026.

Live page: https://rkubler.github.io/shotpeening/

| Simulator | What it simulates | Link |
|---|---|---|
| Shot Peening Coverage Simulator | Local coverage: dents accumulating in a small window under the jet, τ(n) = 100(1 − e^(−Kn)) | https://rkubler.github.io/peening-coverage/ |
| Extended Shot Peening Coverage Simulator | Coverage on a flat part: back-and-forth raster passes with step δ, rasters at 0° and 90°, uniform, Gaussian or flattened Gaussian jet, stripes | https://rkubler.github.io/extended-simulator/ |
| Cylinder Shot Peening Coverage Simulator | Coverage on a rotating cylinder: helix of pitch 60·V/N, back-and-forth passes, curvature, helical stripes | https://rkubler.github.io/cylinder-coverage/ |
| Piezopeening Coverage Simulator | Spherical tool hammering at 100 Hz along CNC lines | https://rkubler.github.io/piezopeening-coverage/ |
